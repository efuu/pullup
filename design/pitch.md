# Application pitch

## PullUp

Making plans with friends is easy. Getting everyone to show up is harder. Someone says “maybe,” someone says “omw” when they haven’t left, and someone is already outside wondering whether anyone else is coming. After a few experiences like that, even the people who used to arrive on time start coming late.

PullUp is a plan page you share in the group chat. It gives everyone one place to check whether the plan is happening, who’s coming, and who’s on their way.

**Decide-by.** Each plan has a deadline to decide, usually a few hours before it starts. Until then, you can say maybe. After the deadline, anyone still undecided isn't counted as coming, but their maybe stays visible, so the organizer can tell a never-decided from a no.

That gives the organizer a headcount they can actually use to book a table or figure out whether the plan is worth keeping. It also gives everyone else a clear moment to commit. Instead of waking up to another “so are we still going?” thread, you can check the page.

**Pulling up / Pulled up.** On the day, there are two buttons: Pulling up and Pulled up. Tap the first when you leave, with an optional estimate of how far away you are. Tap the second when you arrive.

Everyone can see who’s there, who’s on the way and when they left, and who hasn’t said they’ve left yet. If you’re waiting outside, you have a better idea of what to expect. If you’re organizing, you don’t have to text four people separately. And if you’re running late or can't make it, updating your status takes one tap.

**On-time record.** When you arrive after the scheduled start, the page records it as late. Next to your name is a small tag, like “on time 4 of last 5,” visible only to people you’ve shared a plan with.

There are no fines, points, or notifications. The record is meant to address a familiar habit: showing up late because you assume everyone else will. Over time, friends can see whether that assumption is fair. There’s some social pressure in that, intentionally, but it stays within the people you make plans with.

Everything works through a link and a name, no app download and account creation required, reducing the friction needed to use the app.
