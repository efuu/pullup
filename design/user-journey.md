# User journey (beta)

Updated for P3. The alpha journey is in git history; this is what the deployed app does now.

**Who.** Sam usually organizes the group's plans. Last time they went out, Sam held a table at Koreana for fifteen minutes, lost it, and spent another half hour outside texting everyone individually. This Saturday, Sam wants to try KBBQ again without going through all that.

**Trigger.** On Thursday night, Sam texts the group: "KBBQ sat 6?" Three people reply "down," but it's unclear whether everyone else is still deciding or just hasn't checked the chat.

**Step 1: Making the plan.** Sam opens PullUp, types a name (no account), and enters the plan: KBBQ at Koreana, Saturday at 6:00 pm. The decide-by time defaults to 2:00 pm Saturday; Sam leaves it. One tap creates the plan, Sam lands on its page already counted as going, and copies the link into the group chat.

**Step 2: People respond.** Over the next day, Dylan and Priya open the link, enter their names, and tap *Going*; their buttons turn green. Kenny picks *Maybe* (amber), and Alex picks *Can't* (red). Jordan, who has never used PullUp, opens the link and taps *Going*. The page shows "4 going · 1 maybe · 1 can't" with names under each, and next to each name a small tag with their on-time record from earlier plans. Sam can see who has committed and who is still deciding without asking anyone.

**Step 3: The deadline.** At 2:00 pm Saturday, Kenny still hasn't decided. From then on the page reads his answer differently without changing it: the count says "4 going · 1 can't · 1 never decided", Kenny is listed under "Said maybe, never decided" rather than quietly moved to can't, and the Maybe button is greyed out with "past the decide-by time" on it. Sam can see that Kenny was a maybe who never followed up, which is a different thing from Alex, who said no. If Kenny opens the page now, a note tells him he isn't counted and asks for Going or Can't. Sam books a table for four. On Friday Sam had also moved the time from 6:00 to 6:30 using *Change time* under Organizer; everyone's page showed the new time within seconds.

**Step 4: Heading over.** At 6:20 pm, Priya taps *Pulling up* and estimates eight minutes. Dylan taps *Pulling up* at 6:25, then *Pulled up* when he arrives at 6:28. Sam gets there at 6:31 and taps *Pulled up* too. The "Who's where" board shows a green dot for Dylan and Sam, an orange dot for Priya ("8 min away, left 6:20"), and a hollow dot for Jordan ("hasn't left yet"). Priya hits traffic and taps *fix estimate* to say 15 minutes. Sam only needs to text Jordan, who leaves at 6:35 and taps *Pulling up*.

**Step 5: Arriving.** Priya arrives at 6:39 and taps *Pulled up*; her row shows "+9". Jordan arrives at 6:50, "+20". Sam's arrival is one minute late, so "+1"; Dylan's is on time. Each person's tag updates on everyone's page without a refresh: Dylan's now reads "on time 1/1" in green.

**Outcome.** Sam had a real headcount before booking, could see who was coming without chasing anyone in the chat, and spent under a minute in the app. Tapping Jordan's name shows his record ("on time 0 of 1") to anyone who has shared a plan with him, and nobody else. The next time the group makes a plan, that tag is next to his name.

**If things go wrong.** If Sam cancels the plan, everyone's page shows a red banner, the response and day-of controls disappear, and anyone who tries to respond is told the plan isn't taking responses. If someone opens a mistyped link, they get "That plan doesn't exist, or the link is wrong." If a browser's saved identity is lost, the page asks for a name again rather than failing silently.
