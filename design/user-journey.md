# User journey

**Who.** Sam usually organizes the group’s plans. Last time they went out, Sam held a table at Koreana for fifteen minutes, lost it, and spent another half hour outside texting everyone individually. This Saturday, Sam wants to try KBBQ again without going through all that.

**Trigger.** On Thursday night, Sam texts the group: “KBBQ sat 6?” Three people reply “down,” but it’s unclear whether everyone else is still deciding or just hasn’t checked the chat.

**Step 1: Making the plan.** Sam opens PullUp and enters the plan: KBBQ at Koreana, Saturday at 6:00 pm. The deadline to decide defaults to Saturday at 2:00 pm, which works for Sam. One tap creates the plan and copies its link (sketch 1). Sam pastes it into the group chat, then enters a name and taps *Going*.

**Step 2: People respond.** Over the next day, Dylan and Priya open the link, enter their names, and tap *Going*. Kenny picks *Maybe*, and Alex picks *Can’t*. Jordan, who has never used PullUp before, also opens the link and taps *Going*.

The page now shows “4 going, 1 maybe, 1 can’t,” with names under each category (sketch 2). Sam can see who has committed and who is still deciding.

**Step 3: The deadline.** At 2:00 pm Saturday, Kenny still hasn’t decided, so the page counts Kenny as not coming. Four people are going, and Sam books a table for four. Everyone can check the page to see that dinner is still happening.

**Step 4: Heading over.** At 5:50 pm, Priya taps *Pulling up* and estimates eight minutes. Dylan taps *Pulling up* at 5:55, then *Pulled up* when he arrives at 5:58. Sam gets there at 6:01 and taps *Pulled up* too.

The board shows Dylan and Sam at the restaurant, Priya on the way since 5:50 with an original estimate of eight minutes, and Jordan with no departure update yet (sketch 3). Priya is taking longer than expected, but Sam knows she’s on her way. Sam only needs to text Jordan, who leaves at 6:05 and taps *Pulling up*.

**Step 5: Arriving.** Priya arrives at 6:09 and taps *Pulled up*. Jordan arrives at 6:20 and does the same. Their records show nine and twenty minutes late. Sam’s arrival is recorded as one minute late, and Dylan’s as on time (sketch 4).

**Outcome.** Sam had a clear headcount before booking and could see who was coming without chasing everyone in the chat. Using PullUp took only a few brief check-ins.

The next time the group makes a plan, Jordan’s tag reads “on time 0 of 1.” Friends who shared the dinner can see it, and Jordan knows that arriving late has become something the group can actually track.
